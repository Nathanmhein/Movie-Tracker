### Breakdown of activities 

**Activity I - Mock Interview**

- You will be paired with an engineer, who will have a list of technical questions and a feedback guide
- The engineer will ask you several questions from the provided list. The questions will cover different categories, so be prepared for a range of topics. Typically, the interview will start with basic software engineering questions and then progress to more technical ones.
- For the more technical questions, take a moment to gather your thoughts and jot down your ideas before responding. This will help you organize your answers effectively.
- After each question, the engineer will provide you with constructive feedback based on the feedback guide. This feedback will focus on how clearly and confidently you communicate your ideas rather than your technical knowledge.

**Activity II - Pair Programming**

- You will be given a programming problem to solve with your engineer partner, using the driver/navigator system.
- This activity is designed to simulate working with someone you haven't collaborated with before, which is a common scenario in interviews and the workplace.
- The focus is on teamwork, communication, and problem-solving, so work together to find the best solution.
